# Key Insights and Recommendations

## Key Insights

**1. About 1 in 6 customers has left, and it costs the bank revenue.**
1,627 of 10,127 customers (16.1%) churned. They accounted for 11.3% of all card spending and held 15.1% of total credit limit.

**2. Who a customer is matters much less than how they use the card.**
Churn barely differs by age (13.3% to 16.6%), education, marital status or income. Women churn slightly more than men (17.4% vs 14.6%). Platinum cardholders show 25.0% churn but there are only 20 of them. Targeting offers by demographics alone would be ineffective.

**3. Low card usage is the strongest warning sign.**
Customers with 60 or fewer transactions churn at about 31% to 37%, versus under 6% for those with more. Churned customers averaged 44.9 transactions vs 68.7, average spending of 3,095 vs 4,655, and utilisation of 0.16 vs 0.30.

**4. Inactivity and repeated contacts are early warnings.**
Inactive 3+ months: 22.0% churn vs 11.2%. Churn rises from 1.8% with no contacts to 33.5% with five contacts.

**5. Fewer products means a higher chance of leaving.**
1 or 2 products: 25.6% to 27.8% churn. 4 to 6 products: 10.5% to 12.0%.

**6. The biggest spenders are loyal, but a valuable middle group is leaking.**
Spending over 10K: 1.3% churn. The 5K to 10K spending band (1,130 customers) churns at 21.8%. Customers with a credit limit under 2.5K churn at 20.9%.

**7. A simple risk score finds half of all churners in a small group.**
The High risk group (1,648 customers, 16% of the base) churns at 51.1% and holds 842 of 1,627 churners (51.8%). The Low risk group churns at 4.0%.

## Recommendations

| # | Recommendation | Target | Success measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build a monthly High risk call list for the retention team | 1,648 High risk customers | Churn rate of the High group before and after |
| 2 | Re-activate drifting customers with automatic nudges and rewards when card usage drops | Low transactions, inactive 3+ months | Change in transaction count after the offer |
| 3 | Escalate customers with 3+ contacts for first-contact resolution and review why they contact the bank | High contact customers | Churn rate of customers with 3+ contacts |
| 4 | Cross-sell a second or third product | Customers with 1 or 2 products, starting with Medium risk | Share taking a second product, and their churn rate |
| 5 | Protect the 5K to 10K spenders with a personal offer or credit limit review, and review low-limit experience | 5K to 10K spenders, limit under 2.5K | Churn rate in these groups over two quarters |

## Action Plan by Risk Level

| Risk level | Customers | Churn rate | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | 1,648 | 51.1% | Personal call and retention offer, escalate open complaints |
| Medium | 3,483 | 16.8% | Automatic nudges, rewards for card use, cross-sell |
| Low | 4,996 | 4.0% | No special action; keep service quality |

## Example of the opportunity (assumption, not a result)

If a retention programme kept 10% of the 842 High risk churners, about 84 customers would be saved. The 10% is an illustrative assumption. Pilot any action on a small group first and compare results before wider rollout.
